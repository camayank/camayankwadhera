Rules in code. Nothing guessed.

A tax figure has to be the same every time it is worked out. So the arithmetic here is written as rules and tested against hand-worked cases, and each result names the section it comes from.

01

Built for uneven income

Capital gains and dividends are due only from the instalment after they arise. The tools ask when each one happens and apply that rule, which a single yearly estimate cannot do.

02

Shows the cost of being late

Interest for a short or late instalment, and for paying less than 90% in advance, is worked out with the same rounding the law uses.

03

Private by design

The tools run inside your browser. Your income, your statements and your records are never sent to this site or to anyone else. See the privacy page.

04

Honest about its limits

This is software for individuals, resident or non-resident, and it gives an estimate. It does not replace an adviser for losses carried forward, foreign tax credit or a company's advance tax, and it is not a chartered accountant's service.
