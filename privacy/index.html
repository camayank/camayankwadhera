Privacy

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

What this site collects

This site does not set cookies, does not run analytics and does not receive anything you type into it. The company that hosts the site keeps standard server logs of page requests, as every web host does.

The enquiry form

The enquiry form does not send anything by itself. It opens your own email app with a message filled in, and nothing leaves your device until you choose to send that email.

The tax tools

The advance tax tools do all their arithmetic inside your browser. The income figures you enter, and any statement or records you paste or open in the tax statement check, are never sent to this site or to anyone else. So that you do not have to retype them, the figures from the calculator are saved in your own browser's storage on your device. The "Clear everything" link in the calculator removes them, as does clearing your browser's site data. Pasted statements and records are not saved at all.

Emails you send

If you email an enquiry, the details in it are used to reply to you and may be passed to the venture best placed to help, such as CA4CPA, Legal Suvidha, MKW Advisors, DigiComply, CorpReady or GrowBiz. They are not sold or used for unrelated marketing.

Questions and deletion

To ask what is held about you, or to have your enquiry deleted, write to camayankwadhera@gmail.com.
